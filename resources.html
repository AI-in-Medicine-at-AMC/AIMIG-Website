AI TOOLS & RESOURCES

TOOLS AND GUIDES FOR CLINICAL LEARNING, RESEARCH, AND BUILDING WITH AI.

Reviewed November 24, 2026. Check important claims against the original sources. Follow your school’s AI policy and use institution-approved tools for patient information.

Clinical practice
OpenEvidence
  • Search clinical questions and follow citations to guidelines and medical literature
  • Free for verified U.S. clinicians. Medical students can apply through student verification
  • Check the cited source and its applicability to the patient
 Clinical practice
UpToDate Expert AI
  • Ask clinical questions in plain language and follow references to UpToDate topics
  • Access depends on your subscription or institution
 Clinical practice
Abridge
  • Ambient clinical documentation that drafts notes from patient visits for clinician review
  • Use through an approved clinical workflow and follow local consent and documentation policies
 Literature & evidence
PubMed
  • Search biomedical literature directly through the National Library of Medicine
  • Use MeSH terms, filters, and a documented search strategy for reproducible searches
 Literature & evidence
Consensus
  • Ask a research question and get a synthesis with links to the underlying papers
  • Use it to find papers and scope a topic. Check study design and strength of evidence in the original sources
 Literature & evidence
Elicit
  • Find papers and assist with screening and structured data extraction for evidence reviews
  • Verify screening decisions and extracted data against full texts
 Literature & evidence
TRIPOD+AI
  • Reporting checklist for studies that develop or evaluate clinical prediction models
  • Use it to assess how a paper reports its data, methods, and model performance
